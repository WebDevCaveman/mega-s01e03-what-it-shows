The two causes

parser: ticket 1. The shared parser reads every date month first, and our tenants write the day first. A date comes out with day and month swapped. This is what #4471 describes. Fixed: fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales.

hint: ticket 2. The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field and then reads the date the tenant’s way. People type what the hint says, and the date comes out swapped. The symptom matches parser, but the cause is different, so the parser fix does not reach it. Won’t fix: the booking-v3 form is retired.

Do not record all of #4471 as fixed. The parser fix closes `parser` only. `hint` ends because the form is retired, not because the bug was fixed.

How to tell them apart

Look at the tenant and the way in, as src/lib/kb/causes.ts does:

  1. Form report from a tenant on booking-v3: hint.
  2. Any other report from a day-first tenant: parser.

The subject line cannot separate them: both causes use the same subjects, which is how they were merged (decision 0001). The intake tag cannot either. It is a guess from the browser, and the table shows how often it disagrees with the cause.

Count unique report ids. The export pages overlap, so a report can appear twice.
